How the test works

  • Enter your age and the height of your step (15, 20, 25 or 30 cm).
  • Open Fitness Test on your Apple Watch and press Start Test. It reads your starting heart rate and starts the test on your iPhone.
  • Step up-up-down-down to the beat. There are five 2-minute stages, from 15 to 35 steps a minute.
  • Press Record HR on your watch near the end of each stage. If you forget, the app uses your live heart rate and marks it "auto".
  • If your heart rate goes over 80% of your age-predicted maximum, the test stops and you get your score.

Common questions

My watch says "Enter your age in the iPhone app"

Open the app on your iPhone, enter your age, and tap Done on the keyboard. Keep the iPhone app open, then reopen Fitness Test on the watch.

The iPhone says "Install Fitness Test on your Apple Watch"

Open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install next to Fitness Test.

Can I do the test without an Apple Watch?

Yes. Type your starting heart rate under "No watch?", tap Start without watch, and type your heart rate into each stage during the test.

Remove ads isn't working

If Apple asks for your Apple Account password a second time, enter it and tap OK. If you've bought it before, tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

My score didn't appear in Apple Health

Tap Save to Health on the result screen. If nothing happens, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Fitness Test, and allow VO2 Max.

How accurate is the result?

The Chester Step Test gives an estimate, usually within a few ml/kg/min of a laboratory test. It is not a medical assessment. Stop the test if you feel unwell, dizzy or in pain.
